# Classroom Management

Use this file when designing practical execution for a class.

## Proactive Management

- Clear entry task: students know what to do in the first minute.
- Visible agenda: lesson stages and time.
- Short instructions: task, time, output, success criteria.
- Roles: leader, recorder, reporter, materials manager, timekeeper.
- Monitoring route: teacher knows whom to check first.
- Signal: one consistent attention signal or transition phrase.
- Closure: collect evidence before changing stage.

## Common Risks and Moves

| Risk | Prevention | In-class move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| students do not start | model first step, show example | ask one pair to restate task |
| noisy group work | assign roles and output | pause, reset voice level, restart timer |
| only strong students answer | wait time, random/structured response | think-pair-share before whole class |
| weak students hide | scaffold card, paired support | check first item privately |
| time overrun | visible timer, must/optional tasks | skip extension and collect core evidence |
| public lesson silence | accessible first question | give choices or evidence prompt |

## Grouping Patterns

- Pair support: good for quick explanation and weak foundation.
- Mixed group roles: good for project/activity.
- Same-level group: good for targeted remediation or extension.
- Random group: good for low-stakes discussion.
- Teacher-led small group: good for prerequisite gap.

## Online / Hybrid

- checkpoint every 8-12 minutes;
- use polls, shared docs, short response forms;
- ask for product screenshots or one-sentence responses;
- keep instructions shorter than offline;
- prepare fallback if network/device fails.

## Management Script Examples

- "现在用 30 秒看任务，只回答三个问题：做什么、几分钟、交什么。"
- "每组先独立写 1 分钟，再讨论 2 分钟，最后只提交一个共同答案。"
- "如果完成 A 题，继续 B 题；如果 B 题也完成，写一句你发现的规律。"
- "我先看第 2、5、8 组的第一步，其他组按板书步骤继续。"
