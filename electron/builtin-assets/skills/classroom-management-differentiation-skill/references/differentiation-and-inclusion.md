# Differentiation and Inclusion

Use this file for mixed readiness, weak foundation, extension, language support, accessibility, or special support.

## Differentiation Dimensions

- Readiness: prerequisite review, tiered tasks, worked examples.
- Interest: choice of context, product, case, role.
- Process: teacher-led small group, peer support, checklist, manipulatives.
- Product: oral explanation, diagram, written answer, model, slide, code, presentation.
- Support: vocabulary bank, formula card, sentence starters, visual organizer.

## Tiered Support

| Level | Goal | Support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | enter the task and complete core step | example, checklist, vocabulary/formula card |
| Core | meet lesson objective independently | standard task and feedback |
| Extension | transfer or deepen | challenge, counterexample, design, proof, open question |

Do not announce tiers as student labels. Present them as task choices or progression.

## Accessibility and Learner Variability

- Multiple representation: text, diagram, example, model, oral explanation.
- Multiple action/expression: write, draw, speak, demonstrate, build, annotate.
- Multiple engagement: choice, relevance, collaboration, challenge, self-check.

## Support Examples

- Math: worked example, step checklist, error diagnosis table.
- Language: vocabulary bank, sentence frame, evidence sentence starter.
- Science: diagram, variable table, observation template, safety checklist.
- Humanities: timeline, source evidence table, claim-evidence-reasoning frame.
- Technology: starter code, debugging checklist, screen capture proof.

## Sensitive Boundaries

- Do not diagnose disabilities or mental health.
- Use behavior and learning evidence, not labels.
- Protect student privacy.
- For official accommodations, ask for school policy or teacher-provided plan.
