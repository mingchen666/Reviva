# Template: Weekly Report

Use for weekly/monthly progress updates and team sync.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: period, team, owner.
2. Executive snapshot: 3-4 KPIs.
3. Completed work.
4. Current progress by workstream.
5. Risks/blockers.
6. Decisions needed.
7. Next week plan.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L6, L7, L8, L11.

## Theme

Default: Office Focus.

## Notes

Use status colors consistently: green/on track, amber/watch, red/blocked. Do not over-color every cell.
