# Template: Meeting Deck

Use for discussion decks, decision meetings, and working-session materials.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: meeting topic and date.
2. Objective and desired decision.
3. Context recap.
4. Option A / current path.
5. Option B / alternative.
6. Tradeoff matrix.
7. Recommendation.
8. Discussion questions.
9. Decision and next actions.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L3, L5, L8, L10, L11.

## Theme

Default: Office Focus.

## Notes

Make the decision explicit. A meeting deck without a decision slide becomes a memo.
