# Template: Training Deck

Use for corporate training, onboarding, process training, and enablement.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: training topic.
2. Who this is for and expected outcome.
3. Process overview.
4. Step 1.
5. Step 2.
6. Step 3.
7. Example / scenario.
8. Do and don't.
9. Checkpoint.
10. Resources and next action.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L3, L4, L5, L7, L10, L11.

## Theme

Default: Education Clear or Office Focus.
