# Template: Executive Summary

Use for management reviews, business summaries, operating reviews, and board-style updates that are not fundraising pitch decks.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: business/topic and period.
2. One-page executive summary.
3. KPI snapshot.
4. Trend chart or business driver.
5. Key issue or opportunity.
6. Options / recommendation.
7. Risk and mitigation.
8. Decision request / next actions.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L6, L5, L8, L9, L11.

## Theme

Default: Executive Signal.

## Notes

Each slide should make the decision easier. Remove background that does not affect the decision.
