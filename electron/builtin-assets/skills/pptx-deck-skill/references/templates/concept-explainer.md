# Template: Concept Explainer

Use for explaining principles, science, technical concepts, or "how X works".

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: concept and promise.
2. Why it matters.
3. Mental model or analogy.
4. Mechanism diagram.
5. Step-by-step process.
6. Example in context.
7. Misconception or boundary.
8. Summary.
9. Optional: companion animation suggestion.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L3, L4, L5, L7, L11.

## Theme

Default: Education Clear or Study Calm.

## AI Animation Pairing

If the explanation is about process, architecture, network layers, algorithms, biology mechanisms, or physical systems, offer/produce an HTML animation companion with `ai-animation-skill` when requested.
