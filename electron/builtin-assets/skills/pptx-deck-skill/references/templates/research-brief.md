# Template: Research Brief

Use for desk research, competitor analysis, market scan, source synthesis, and literature summaries.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: research question.
2. Scope and method.
3. Key finding summary.
4. Evidence 1.
5. Evidence 2.
6. Comparison matrix.
7. Implications.
8. Open questions.
9. Recommendation or next research.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L5, L6, L9, L11.

## Theme

Default: Research Brief.

## Notes

Never fabricate citations. If source details are missing, say so in notes or final response.
