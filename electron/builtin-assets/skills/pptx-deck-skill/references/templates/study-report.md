# Template: Study Report

Use for book reports, paper reading, learning summaries, and study sharing.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: topic/source and presenter.
2. Why this material matters.
3. Structure of the material.
4. Key idea 1 with evidence.
5. Key idea 2 with example.
6. Key idea 3 with implication.
7. Personal synthesis or comparison.
8. Questions for discussion.
9. Takeaways and next reading.

## Recommended Layouts

L1, L3, L5, L9, L10, L11.

## Theme

Default: Study Calm or Research Brief.
