# Template: Exam Review

Use for review decks, knowledge organization, and exam preparation.

## Typical Arc

1. Cover: exam/topic scope.
2. Review map: chapters or knowledge areas.
3. High-frequency concepts.
4. Formula/rule sheet with meaning, not just symbols.
5. Typical question pattern 1.
6. Worked solution.
7. Common mistakes.
8. Typical question pattern 2.
9. Quick self-test.
10. Final checklist.

## Recommended Layouts

L2, L3, L4, L5, L10, L11.

## Theme

Default: Study Calm.

## Notes

Put answer explanations in notes or follow-up slides. Do not hide essential knowledge only in animation.
